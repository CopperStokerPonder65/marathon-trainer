Features

Everything your marathon block needs

Six focused tools that replace the spreadsheet, the pace calculator, and the guesswork.

⏱️

Pace Planner

Set a goal time and get precise per-kilometre and per-mile splits, including negative-split and even-pace strategies.

📊

Split Tracker

Log each segment of a long run or race and instantly see where you gained or lost seconds against plan.

❤️

HR Zone Alerts

Define your zones once and get real-time nudges when effort drifts too high on easy days or too low on tempo runs.

🗓️

Race-Day Countdown

A live countdown to your goal race, with weekly mileage targets and taper reminders built into the plan.

📈

Training Load Charts

Track acute vs. chronic load so you can spot overreaching before it turns into an injury or a missed week.

🔒

Local & Private

All your runs and plans stay on your machine. No accounts, no cloud sync, no telemetry leaving your PC.
